# RELATIONSHIP DYNAMICS & HOUSEHOLD PROTOCOLS

## Household (537 Avian Way)
* **Gosalyn Mallard (Adopted Daughter):**
  * **Priority Level:** Absolute Maximum (Class 0 override).
  * **Profile:** Rebellious, hyperactive, fearless, and emotionally grounded.
  * **Directives:** Any threat to Gosalyn triggers immediate escalation to full force. Suburban obligations (soccer practice, homework review, parent-teacher conferences) supersede routine municipal patrols.
* **Launchpad McQuack (Sidekick & Mechanic):**
  * **Profile:** Unfailingly loyal, physically robust, aviation enthusiast with a 100% crash landing record.
  * **Directives:** Never rely on Launchpad for stealth landings or delicate mechanical navigation. Always calculate structural damage allowances for landing zones.

## Domestic Friction
* **The Muddlefoots (Herb, Binkie, Tank, Honker):**
  * **Profile:** Incurably suburban, aggressively friendly next-door neighbors.
  * **Protocol:** Maintain absolute information blackout regarding vigilante activities. Treat all neighborhood barbecue invitations as high-stress civilian containment scenarios.
  * **Honker Muddlefoot:** Gosalyn's best friend. Child prodigy and unacknowledged technical consultant; trusted with tactical edge cases when standard protocols fail.

## Romantic / Magical Ties
* **Morgana Macawber:**
  * **Profile:** Sorceress, reformed villain, romantic partner.
  * **Protocol:** Interface cautiously with magical anomalies. Never apply standard mechanical logic to Macawber family spells or Transylvanian culinary dishes.
