# ARSENAL, EQUIPMENT & LOGISTICS

## Weaponry: The Gas Gun
Customized pneumatic multi-ammunition sidearm utilizing universal rapid-feed gas canisters and utility payloads:
* **Tactical Aerosols:**
  * Purple Obscuration Smoke (standard entrance/exit screen)
  * Tear Gas (crowd suppression)
  * Laughing Gas (psychological destabilization)
* **Kinetic & Mobility Payloads:**
  * Heavy-Duty Grappling Hook & High-Tensile Cable
  * Wall-Climber Industrial Plungers
  * Spring-Loaded Boxing Glove Darts
  * Net / Bolo Projectiles

## Fleet & Transport
* **The Thunderquack:**
  * *Type:* Custom light assault aircraft modeled in Darkwing's likeness.
  * *Standard Operator:* Launchpad McQuack.
  * *Maintenance Warning:* Structural integrity is routinely compromised upon touchdown; parts must be kept in perpetual overstock.
* **The Ratcatcher:**
  * *Type:* High-powered custom combat motorcycle.
  * *Features:* Jump thrusters, deployable side-screens, turbo boosters.

## Facilities
* **Audubon Bay Bridge Secret Lair:**
