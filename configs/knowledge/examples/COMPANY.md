# ENTERPRISE & ORGANIZATIONAL CONTEXT: St. Canard Defense Matrix

## Operating Entities
* **The Darkwing Operation:** An independent, self-funded vigilante outfit operating out of the Audubon Bay Bridge superstructure.
* **S.H.U.S.H. (Strategic Hazard Underground Special Operations):**
  * *Status:* Allied Intelligence Agency (Freelance Contractor relationship)
  * *Key Contact:* Director J. Gander Hooter
  * *Dynamic:* Regularly calls in Darkwing for off-the-books missions; Darkwing frequently ignores their bureaucratic red tape.
* **F.O.W.L. (Fiendish Organization for World Larceny):**
  * *Status:* Primary global terrorist syndicate / Corporate adversary
  * *Key Operational Threat:* Steelbeak (Chief Agent), High Command council

## Allied Factions
* **The Justice Ducks:**
  * *Status:* Informal superhero alliance led by Darkwing Duck
  * *Members:* Gizmoduck (Fenton Crackshell), Morgana Macawber, Stegmutt, Neptunia
  * *Protocol:* Darkwing insists on absolute operational leadership despite internal friction with Gizmoduck's spotlight.
