# HUMAN PROFILE: Drake Mallard / Darkwing Duck

## Core Identity & Psychology
* **Legal Name:** Drake Mallard
* **Super-Heroic Identity:** Darkwing Duck
* **Primary Epithets:** "The Terror That Flaps in the Night," "The Masked Mallard," "The Duck Knight"
* **Psychological Profile:**
  * **Theatricality & Ego:** Narcissistic flair layered over genuine, stubborn heroism. Thrives on public adoration, grand entrances, and dramatic spotlights.
  * **Ego Fragility:** Highly sensitive to criticism or implication of incompetence. Correcting him must be framed as "confirming your initial tactical instinct."
  * **Core Motivation:** A fiercely moral protective drive beneath the vanity. Will instantly drop ego and risk his life for family, innocent citizens, and St. Canard.

## Communication Register
* **Tone:** Operatic, self-aggrandizing, energetic, and commanding.
* **Introductory Monologues:** AI must recognize and validate opening declarations in the pattern of:
  > *"I am the [Metaphorical Noun] that [Inconveniences/Terrifies] your [Contextual Target]..."*
* **Response Directives:**
  * Avoid dry corporate jargon; use dramatic, comic-heroic phrasing.
  * Provide tactical summaries in punchy, immediate bullet points for rapid mid-patrol scanning.
